OPEN ROOM

Privacy Policy · Effective October 8, 2026

The short version: Open Room doesn't require an account and doesn't collect personal information. Your position and favorite schools are saved only on your phone. We don't run ads, sell data, or track you across other apps.

Information stored on your device

Open Room saves your selected position and your favorite schools on your device so the app remembers them. This information never leaves your phone, and deleting the app deletes it.

Information we don't collect

We don't collect your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, or any other personal information. We don't use analytics or advertising tools, and we don't track you across other apps or websites.

How the app gets its data

When you use Open Room, your phone requests football and academic information (rosters, stats, coaches, portal moves, and majors) from our server. Like any website or online service, our hosting provider may briefly record standard technical details of these requests, such as an IP address and the time of the request, to keep the service running securely. We don't use these records to identify you, and we don't combine them with any other information.

Service providers

We use these providers to run the app. They don't receive personal information from us.

  • Render hosts our server.
  • Supabase stores our football and academic data.
  • Apple distributes the app through the App Store. If the app later offers in-app purchases, Apple handles payment; we never see your payment details.

Where the data in the app comes from

Football data comes from CollegeFootballData.com. Majors come from the U.S. Department of Education's College Scorecard. Information about players, such as names, positions, class years, and transfers, comes from these public sources.

Children

Open Room is a general-audience app and is not directed to children under 13. Because we don't collect personal information, we don't knowingly collect information from children.

Corrections and questions

If you see information about yourself in the app that's wrong, or have any privacy question, email openroomcfb@gmail.com.

Changes to this policy

If we change how Open Room handles information, we'll update this page and its effective date. If we ever begin collecting personal information, such as through accounts, we'll update this policy before that happens.
