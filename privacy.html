OPEN ROOM

Privacy Policy · Effective October 23, 2026

The short version: Open Room (the iPhone app and the Open Room website) doesn't require an account. The app and website don't collect personal information unless you choose to email us or sign up for the coach pilot. Your selections are saved only on your device. We don't run ads, sell data, or track you across other apps or websites.

Information stored on your device

Open Room saves your selected position and your favorite schools on your device so the app remembers them. This information never leaves your phone, and deleting the app deletes it.

The Open Room website

The website saves your selections, such as your coach target board, in your browser's storage on your device. This information isn't sent to us, and clearing your browser data deletes it. The website is hosted by GitHub Pages. Like any website host, GitHub may record standard technical details of visits, such as an IP address, to keep the site running securely.

Coach pilot signup

If you sign up for the coach pilot, the form opens your own email app with a message addressed to us. Nothing is sent until you send that email. The message includes the information you entered: your name, role, school or program, email address, and, if you provide them, your phone number and notes. We use it only to contact you about the pilot and Open Room's coach tools, and we don't sell or share it. To have your information deleted, email openroomcfb@gmail.com.

Information we don't collect

Unless you choose to email us or sign up for the coach pilot, we don't collect your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, or any other personal information. We don't use analytics or advertising tools, and we don't track you across other apps or websites.

How the app and website get their data

When you use Open Room, your phone or browser requests football and academic information (rosters, stats, coaches, portal moves, and majors) from our server. Like any website or online service, our hosting provider may briefly record standard technical details of these requests, such as an IP address and the time of the request, to keep the service running securely. We don't use these records to identify you, and we don't combine them with any other information.

Service providers

We use these providers to run the app and website. They don't receive personal information from us.

  • Render hosts our server.
  • Supabase stores our football and academic data.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the Open Room website.
  • Apple distributes the app through the App Store. If the app later offers in-app purchases, Apple handles payment; we never see your payment details.

Where the data in Open Room comes from

Football data comes from CollegeFootballData.com. Majors come from the U.S. Department of Education's College Scorecard. Information about players, such as names, positions, class years, and transfers, comes from these public sources.

Children

Open Room is a general-audience app and website and is not directed to children under 13. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children.

Corrections and questions

If you see information about yourself in Open Room that's wrong, or have any privacy question, email openroomcfb@gmail.com.

Changes to this policy

If we change how Open Room handles information, we'll update this page and its effective date. If we ever begin collecting personal information, such as through accounts, we'll update this policy before that happens.
