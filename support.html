What Open Room does

Pick your position and major to see every FBS and FCS school ranked by how much of your position room opens up next season. Open any school for its roster, head coach, player game logs, majors, and last portal cycle. Explore the same data on the website at openroomcfb.com.

Common questions

How is "leaving" counted?
A player counts as leaving if he's listed as a senior or later. It's an estimate based on class year, not a confirmed departure. Always confirm with the program.

Why is portal data limited for some FCS schools?
Public transfer data for FCS programs is less complete than for FBS. Schools with limited portal data are labeled in the app.

How often is the data updated?
Rosters and stats update twice a week during the season.

Why don't I see my exact major?
Majors are listed by program family, so one entry can cover several related fields. Check the school's catalog for exact degree names.

Do I need an account?
No. Without one, your position and favorites are saved on your device. An account lets your settings sync between the app and the website, and it's required to buy Pro.

How do I delete my account?
In the app, go to your profile and tap Delete Account. On the website, go to your account page and choose Delete Account. You can also email support@openroomcfb.com from the address on your account. If you have a Pro subscription, also cancel it in your iPhone's Settings, since Apple manages billing.

I didn't get my confirmation email.
Check your spam or promotions folder for an email from Open Room. If it isn't there, email us and we'll help.
