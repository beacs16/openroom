What Open Room does

Pick your position and major to see every FBS school ranked by how much of your position room opens up next season. Open any school for its roster, head coach, player game logs, majors, and last portal cycle.

Common questions

How is "leaving" counted?
A player counts as leaving if he's listed as a senior or later. It's an estimate based on class year, not a confirmed departure. Always confirm with the program.

How often is the data updated?
Rosters and stats update twice a week during the season.

Why don't I see my exact major?
Majors are listed by program family, so one entry can cover several related fields. Check the school's catalog for exact degree names.

Do I need an account?
No. Your position and favorites are saved only on your phone.
